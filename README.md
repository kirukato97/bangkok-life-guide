# Bangkok Bound

Create a modern, high-converting single-page landing page in Russian for a Bangkok relocation consulting service.
Core Positioning: The service focuses exclusively on relocating to Bangkok (not Phuket or Pattaya). Bangkok is positioned as a real city for long-term living, in contrast to popular beach destinations that often feel like extended resorts with repetitive social circles, the same faces, and a constant tourist/party atmosphere.
About the Service Provider:
Has significant experience living in Bangkok
Knows local legislation, practical processes, and the language
Has established local connections and working systems
Ready to provide effective, on-the-ground support
Target Audience: Russian-speaking individuals (primarily ages 28–45), including remote workers, IT professionals, and families who are considering alternative places to live due to ongoing economic and social challenges in their home country, and who want a functional city for actual living rather than a permanent vacation.
Page Structure:
Hero Section
Strong headline
Clear subheadline
Primary call-to-action button (e.g., “Leave a request” / “Message on Telegram”)
Problem Section
Subtly address the current reality: many people are re-evaluating their long-term prospects due to economic instability, social tension, and general uncertainty in their home country.
Avoid direct political language, dramatic wording, or fear-mongering.
Frame it as a calm, rational search for more stable and predictable living conditions.
Additionally explain why beach destinations (Phuket, Pattaya, etc.) often become repetitive after a few months: limited social circles, same environments, and a tourist-oriented lifestyle.
Solution Section – Why Bangkok
Highlight Bangkok’s advantages for long-term living: relative stability, developed infrastructure, public transport, healthcare, food variety, diverse neighborhoods, greater anonymity, and a genuine urban lifestyle.
Why Work With Us
Deep local experience in Bangkok
Knowledge of legislation and practical processes
Established connections and working systems
Ability to provide real on-the-ground support
Services
Visa guidance and support
Housing search
Bank account, insurance, and basic settling-in assistance
On-the-ground coordination
How It Works
Simple 3–4 step process
Final Call-to-Action
Clear invitation to contact via Telegram
Short supporting text
Footer Disclaimer
Note that visa decisions are made by Thai immigration authorities and results cannot be guaranteed.
Tone & Style Requirements:
Direct, honest, and calm
Professional but approachable
No hype, exaggerated promises, aggressive sales language, or yellow-press style
Avoid explicit political references
Clean and modern visual design
Fully responsive (mobile-first)
High readability and clear visual hierarchy
Focused on conversion (contact / Telegram)
Technical Requirements:
Deliver clean, well-structured HTML + CSS
Minimal JavaScript only if necessary
Modern, neutral color scheme
Fast-loading and easy to maintain
Please generate the complete landing page code.

This version introduces the pain point carefully and stays within safer boundaries.
